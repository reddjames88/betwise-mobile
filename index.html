Model tuning

Vig is only used when a leg has no two-way market to de-vig. Fair prices otherwise come from the power method across up to 10 books, weighted toward sharp books (Pinnacle). Correlation raises the joint chance of legs that win together (a QB's yards and his team's total, two overs from the same player). Same-game pricing cuts the estimated payout by the same correlation the book charges for, plus its extra SGP margin, so correlated combos don't look like fake value. Stale edges: a leg that beats the market by more than this is usually a stale or mismatched line, so its edge is capped and flagged ⚠. Search depth is how many top legs the optimizer combines (higher = slower but more thorough).

🧠 IQ Mode & Claude analyst V5

IQ Mode (free, no key needed) skips legs that hit 1 or fewer of their last 5 on that exact line, players ESPN lists as Out or Doubtful, and stale lines. It moves each leg's win % for recent form and ESPN's matchup projection (never more than 10 points off the market), and ranks parlays for high hit % at a price no shorter than your IQ min price. Lotto tickets keep their own payout target. Ask Claude sends the slate, the leg pool and the current builds to Claude, which researches the games, flags legs to avoid and builds its own parlays from your pool. BetWise prices them with the same model. A deep analysis typically costs about $0.30–$1.50 on your Anthropic account. Your key is saved only on this device and is sent only to Anthropic.

Last-5 form Free player stats from ESPN, no credits used

Every player prop shows how the player did against that exact line in their last 5 games (fewer early in a season, reaching back into last season when needed). The market price stays the anchor and recent form nudges it: on Balanced, a 5-for-5 streak on a 50% line moves it to about 56%, and 0-for-5 to about 44%. Heavy trusts streaks more, Light less. Lines already price in a lot of recent form, which is why five games only get a nudge.
